podemos falar assim tbm:
**PAST** – for minutes after the hour *(até 30)*  
**TO** – for minutes before the next hour *(31–59)*

**3:10** – Ten past three  
**3:15** – A quarter past three



*commonly used in British English*

**PAST** (for minutes 1-30): You say the minutes first, then "past" and the hour

- 3:10 = "Ten past three"
- 3:15 = "A quarter past three"
- 3:30 = "Half past three"

**TO** (for minutes 31-59): You say how many minutes are left until the next hour, then "to" and that next hour

- 3:45 = "A quarter to four" (15 minutes to 4:00)
- 3:50 = "Ten to four" (10 minutes to 4:00)
- 3:55 = "Five to four" (5 minutes to 4:00)

The key terms to remember:

- **Quarter** = 15 minutes
- **Half** = 30 minutes
